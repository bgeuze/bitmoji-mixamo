Mixamo clips on your player's Bitmoji

Snapchat's get3DAvatar() gives you a rigged Bitmoji with no animations. Drop Mixamo FBX files here and you get one small .glb that bitmoji-anim.js plays on it in your PlayCanvas Web Lens. Everything runs in this page; no file leaves your computer.

  1. 1Download clips from Mixamo as FBX, “Without Skin”
  2. 2Drop them here and name them
  3. 3Put animations.glb in your lens and call play()
